\begin{abstract}
For any smooth, closed, orientable manifold~$M$, the mapping class group~$\operatorname{Mod}(M)$ is the group of orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms of~$M$, up to isotopy. The Nielsen realization problem asks which finite subgroups of~$\operatorname{Mod}(M)$ can be lifted to finite groups of diffeomorphisms.  In this talk we will study a variant of this problem, in which we investigate lifting finite subgroups of the outer automorphism group of the fundamental group of~$M$ to~$\operatorname{Mod}(M)$.  In most cases, we find a complete set of cohomological obstructions that determine whether such a lift exists. This is joint work with B.~Tshishiku.
\end{abstract}
