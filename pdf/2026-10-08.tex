\begin{abstract}
This talk is about preliminary results in a joint work with Raphael Bennett-Tennenhausen and Baptiste Rognerud.
Inspired by the settings of TDA, we generalize the definition of ``representation finite type'' to infinite (even uncountable!) posets. The definition is, more in general, given for appropriately nice categories, as we'll discuss in the talk. We'll present several examples, mostly from TDA, as well as relation with other definitions from the literature also generalizing the notion of RF type.
While introducing some non-examples, we'll also discuss an (almost-completed) characterization of SRF posets.

Even if the examples and motivation for this work come from TDA, the project (and talk) is centered in representation theory and combinatorics of infinite posets.
\end{abstract}
