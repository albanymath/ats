\begin{abstract}
We will discuss the notion of a vector field on a chain complex, and how that relates to the classical notion of a vector field on a smooth manifold. This leads to the somewhat surprising and fun fact that among all the possible ways to triangulate the prism~$\Delta^n \times I$ (where $\Delta^n$ is the standard $n$-simplex and $I$ is the unit interval), there is exactly one triangulation that is more equal than the others. This talk will be very accessible to graduate students.
\end{abstract}
