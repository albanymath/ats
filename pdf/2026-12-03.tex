\begin{abstract}
Root of unity quantum cluster algebras have distinguished central subalgebras that are the images of the quantum Frobenius map. We will describe an explicit classification of the corresponding relative automorphism groups (Frobenius automorphism groups), as well as the relative automorphism groups with respect to their full centers (the ozone groups of Chan–Gaddis–Won–Zhang). Ozone groups have attracted substantial interest recently, but explicit classifications have been obtained only in very restrictive situations. Many important quantum algebras arise as special cases of these algebras; our results give an explicit way to compute their Frobenius and ozone automorphism groups.
\end{abstract}
