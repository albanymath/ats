\begin{abstract}
Quantum Toda lattice is ubiquitous in modern representation theory. To name a very few applications, it appears in Langlands duality, mirror symmetry, Coulomb branches, and so on. In this talk, I will tell about various avatars and their interplay of its $2\times2$ Lax matrix realization focusing primarily on (degenerate) affine Hecke algebra, shuffle algebras, and (shifted) Yangians, as well as indicate their conjectural connections to purely algebro-geometric constructions such as bundles on elliptic curve and Fourier-Mukai duality.
\end{abstract}
