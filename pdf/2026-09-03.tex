\begin{abstract}
The $n$-th (unordered) configuration space of a space~$X$ is the collection of sets of $n$ distinct points in~$X$. We introduce select tools used to compute the (co)homology of configuration spaces of graphs and manifolds, with an emphasis on compactifications of these spaces. Particular topics include (co)homological stability and scanning maps. This expository talk is inspired by the Configuration Spaces Summer School at the University of Michigan, organized by Jeremy Miller and Jenny Wilson, which I attended in May.
\end{abstract}
